# Camille Dubois

Role: EMEA regional leader.

Camille's recorded portfolio connects [[regional-launch-readiness]] with [[meridian-strategic-account]]. The regional view needs to show where a global plan meets local customer, language, channel, approval, or support requirements.

## Meridian discussion
The proposed account evaluation lets operations staff find approved policy information. It is bounded to manuals, escalation guides, FAQs, and revision notices.

Regional coordination should expose local needs affecting that document collection or its support route. It should not turn a limited account evaluation into an assumed rollout across the region. Named customer approvers and deployment commitments remain unresolved.

## Useful handoff
Bring the global decision, its local effect, and the specific question that needs coordination. Separate material delivered centrally from material accepted locally.

Use [[weekly-leadership-staff]] to settle cross-team questions and [[decision-log]] to record agreements. Camille's role provides regional context; it does not replace customer or technical approval.
