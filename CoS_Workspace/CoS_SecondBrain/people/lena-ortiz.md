# Lena Ortiz

Role: People and talent leader.

Lena connects [[workforce-plan]] with [[leadership-succession]]. Start by defining the capability the business needs and the work left uncovered. Hiring is one option to compare.

## Coverage discussion
The current planning questions concern enterprise onboarding and partner enablement. Options include an internal development assignment, reassignment, staged work, or recruitment.

Compare urgency, the durability of the need, and the coaching or onboarding capacity available. A new role takes time to become productive; an internal assignment also needs support.

## Keep personal assessments separate
[[executive-recruiting]] holds role and interview questions. Succession work should distinguish temporary coverage from readiness for a permanent role. These notes do not name candidates, assess employees, or approve hiring.

Bring the business consequence of the gap to [[weekly-leadership-staff]], and record agreed actions in [[decision-log]] at the appropriate level of access.
