# Noah Williams

Noah leads global sales operations. He is accountable for [[global-sales-forecast|the global sales forecast]], with dependencies on [[supply-allocation-plan|the supply allocation plan]].

## Explaining a forecast change
A revised forecast can reflect a change in customer scope, purchase timing, probability, or interpretation of the same customer signal. The review needs the reason for the change and the customer milestone that supports it.

Show value, timing, and confidence separately. A revised total alone can hide a delay or a weaker assumption.

## Shared risks
Several opportunities may depend on the same supply or deployment capacity. Call out those shared dependencies so reviewers can see where one problem could affect multiple forecasts.

A requested shipment or an executive conversation is not confirmation of a purchase. Connect the forecast to the supply plan while keeping that uncertainty visible. Bring the outcome, risk, and required decision to [[weekly-leadership-staff|weekly leadership staff]], then record commitments in the [[decision-log|decision log]].
