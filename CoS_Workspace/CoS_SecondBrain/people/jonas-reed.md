# Jonas Reed

Role: Developer ecosystem lead.

Jonas connects [[developer-ecosystem-expansion]] and [[global-developer-conference]]. The goal is to help a developer get from first interest to a useful example and a clear support route.

## Proposed learning package
An introductory workshop leads into a guided integration example, troubleshooting clinic, and support handoff. Materials under discussion include a setup checklist, participant guide, annotated example, diagnostic worksheet, and maintained support reference.

Review where participants could get stuck: permissions, missing prerequisites, debugging, outdated configuration, or unclear ownership of the instructions.

## What success needs to mean
Attendance alone is not enough. Ask whether participants can follow the example, understand its limits, and find help afterward. Also establish who would maintain the materials when things change.

[[partner-certification]] is a separate evidence review, not an automatic outcome of teaching the example. Coordinate next decisions through [[weekly-leadership-staff]] and [[decision-log]].
