# Ethan Brooks

Role: Supply and allocation leader.

Ethan's recorded portfolio connects [[supply-allocation-plan]] and [[regional-launch-readiness]]. A supply request needs more than a quantity: the team needs to understand when it is needed, whether the recipient is ready, and what happens if it arrives later.

## Information for the discussion
Bring confirmed obligations, available supply, uncertainty in incoming deliveries, and possible substitutes. Separate a requested allocation from a promised one.

An urgent exception can displace another commitment. Explain that cost and record the reason so a one-time intervention does not quietly become a standing priority.

## Questions worth resolving
Could sequencing or substitution meet the need? Does a region's plan assume supply that is still uncertain? Is the customer ready to use what is being requested?

Coordinate those questions through [[weekly-leadership-staff]] and preserve accepted decisions in [[decision-log]].
