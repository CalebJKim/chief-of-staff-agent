# Leila Morgan

Role: Pricing strategy director.

Leila's main recorded responsibility is [[pricing-and-packaging]], connected to [[finance-operating-plan]]. The discussion needs to explain what the customer receives and what it takes to deliver and support it, alongside the price.

## Material for discussion
Explain the customer segment, buying process, included capabilities, support, upgrade path, and partner responsibilities. Compare alternatives and show the assumptions behind the economics.

A lower entry price could simplify adoption but create a harder upgrade or a support burden. A broad bundle could simplify buying while including things some customers do not need.

Keep exploration of willingness to pay separate from an approved offer. Confirm the effect on operating costs before presenting a package as sustainable.

Use [[weekly-leadership-staff]] for unresolved cross-team choices and [[decision-log]] for agreements.
