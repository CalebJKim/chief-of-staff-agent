# Sarah Bennett

Sarah leads board operations. She is accountable for [[board-readiness-program|the board readiness program]] and works with [[communications-readiness|communications readiness]].

## Building the board package
Inputs from different functions need consistent definitions, reporting periods, and decision requests. Otherwise, slides that look compatible may describe different periods or measure different things.

Distinguish information the board needs for oversight from a request that needs an explicit board decision. Show unresolved assumptions and what would happen if a decision were deferred.

## Sharing the story
Communications coordination helps teams describe the same situation consistently. Board-sensitive detail may still need to stay out of externally shared material. Check the disclosure limits for each audience.

Bring the outcome, risks, and required decisions to [[weekly-leadership-staff|weekly leadership staff]]. Record commitments in the [[decision-log|decision log]].
