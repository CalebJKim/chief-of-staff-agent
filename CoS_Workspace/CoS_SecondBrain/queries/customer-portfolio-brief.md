# Customer Portfolio Brief

The accounts need different kinds of executive attention.

- [[northstar-systems|Northstar Systems]] needs a recovery plan and decisions about deployment sequencing.
- [[meridian-financial|Meridian Financial]] needs a reference architecture and governance plan before the next commitment.
- [[summit-retail-group|Summit Retail Group]] needs a final experience owner for retail readiness.

Use [[executive-sponsorship|executive sponsorship]] to identify decisions beyond routine account management.

## Current discussion material
| Account | Concrete working focus | Pending evidence or decision |
|---|---|---|
| Meridian Financial | Internal policy-reference evaluation for operations, with source citations and a bounded document collection | Authoritative versions, access mapping, acceptance questions, and support ownership |
| Northstar Systems | Implementation-to-support handoff covering configuration, limitations, and case routing | Shared checklist review and customer confirmation of the support route |
| Summit Retail Group | Existing retail readiness and experience-owner discussion | The recorded ownership question remains unresolved |

Meridian's proposed evaluation covers a bounded collection of policy documents for internal operations staff, with citations to the sources. It excludes transaction processing and customer-facing financial advice.

Northstar's checklist addresses a support-handoff gap. The handoff still needs review and customer confirmation. Summit's ownership question also remains unresolved.

## Preparing the review
For each account, show the customer's desired outcome, the unresolved dependency, and the decision that needs senior involvement. Keep technical progress, commercial commitments, and customer confirmation separate.

Lessons across accounts can improve onboarding material and [[enterprise-support-model|the enterprise support model]]. Retain each account's acceptance conditions: reduced disruption, an accepted evaluation, and a repeatable store experience require different evidence. This is planning context, not a record of new commitments or completed work.
