# Partner Ecosystem Map

- [[vector-labs|Vector Labs]] contributes software-partnership and launch context.
- [[arcadia-cloud|Arcadia Cloud]] covers hybrid integration and resilience discussions.
- [[brightforge-studios|Brightforge Studios]] supports creative production.
- [[summit-retail-group|Summit Retail Group]] supports the retail experience.

Coordination runs through [[hermes-partner-program|the Hermes partner program]] and [[partner-certification|partner certification]].

## Preparing a joint discussion
Identify what passes between organizations: technical interfaces, source material, support responsibilities, customer commitments, or public wording. Explain when a delay in one relationship affects another.

The proposed education packet includes a workflow explanation, integration outline, participant material, diagnostic worksheet, and support handoff. Certification separately reviews reproducible setup, permissions, failure handling, and maintenance.

## Keeping responsibilities clear
The Arcadia discussion concerns document-source connectivity, corrections, revoked access, and incident routing. Meridian's policy-reference workflow is a possible discussion case; Arcadia has not been selected as its supplier.

Vector's existing software relationship does not establish a new contribution or service obligation. Brightforge's production work and Summit's retail preparation need their own evidence and agreements, separate from integration certification.

A compatibility check, an approved creative asset, and a store-ready experience represent different kinds of progress. Keep exploratory collaboration, accepted obligations, and permission to make joint claims distinct.
