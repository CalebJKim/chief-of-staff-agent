# Arcadia Cloud

Arcadia is a cloud-integration partner in the wider ecosystem. The working discussion concerns how a document source connects to an application, and where the provider's responsibilities end and application operations or support take over.

Meridian's proposed policy-reference workflow is a useful discussion example. This does not establish Arcadia as a selected supplier for that account.

## What the interface discussion needs
Describe how the source is identified, how credentials and permissions are managed, how failures appear, and what information can be shared for diagnosis.

The responsibility map also needs to cover document corrections, access revocation, saved copies, and user-facing error messages. Otherwise both teams may assume the other handles the same issue.

## Proposed walkthrough
Work through an unavailable source, a revoked permission, and a superseded procedure. For each, identify:
- The first symptom the user or operator sees.
- Who receives the problem.
- What evidence is needed to investigate it.
- Who can make the correction.
- How the team confirms the issue is resolved.

Use [[operational-resilience]] and [[data-governance-program]] to keep recovery and information-handling questions connected. [[partner-certification]] covers a separate review of scoped integration evidence.

The intended result is an agreed responsibility map and a list of unresolved questions in [[decision-log]], not a claim of completed validation or a new availability guarantee.
