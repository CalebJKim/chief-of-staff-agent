# Northstar Systems

Northstar is an enterprise customer with an unresolved deployment and support handoff. The immediate coordination problem is a missing shared checklist: configuration context, known limitations, and case-routing responsibilities are not yet collected into one agreed record.

This can make a simple operational question difficult to resolve because the receiving team lacks the context or authority to answer it.

## What the customer needs
The proposed handoff material should explain:
- Where to raise a support question.
- What implementation follow-up still covers.
- Which limitations or workarounds remain.
- How an access request differs from an incident or a design change.
- How incorrect or missing information gets corrected.

The customer-facing view should explain the outcome and next step, not expose the customer to a list of internal team transfers.

## What acceptance would mean
Implementation and support need to agree on the checklist and routing. The customer then needs to confirm that the support path is understandable and that any limitations are visible. An internal review alone would not establish that acceptance.

No completed recovery, customer sign-off, or new service-level promise is recorded by this note.
