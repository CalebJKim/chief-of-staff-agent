# Global Analyst Council

The council is a priority analyst community for strategy, differentiation, and roadmap briefings. Engagement runs through [[analyst-relations-program|the analyst relations program]] and informs [[competitive-response-deepseek|the competitive response to DeepSeek]].

## Preparing a useful conversation
Analyst questions can reveal how external observers group the market, compare alternatives, and interpret demand. They may expose unclear positioning or missing evidence.

Tailor the material to the analyst's coverage. Capture challenges and unanswered questions alongside positive reactions. Participation in a briefing does not imply endorsement, and exploratory feedback is not a published conclusion.

## Proposed briefing material
The working packet contains a workflow overview, terminology guide, source-and-disclosure checklist, and questions for follow-up research.

Different conversations may emphasize different parts:
- Strategy: positioning and adoption.
- Operations: integration and ownership.
- Governance: permissions, inspection, and correction.

Keep the factual basis consistent across those versions. Separate publicly supportable statements from topics needing disclosure review.

## Follow-up
Distinguish a request for clarification from disagreement and from a claim needing verification. Track research through the analyst program and wording changes through [[communications-readiness|communications readiness]].

Maintain one accountable sponsor, a written outcome, and a dated next decision in the [[decision-log|decision log]]. No analyst statements, endorsements, publication plans, or meeting outcomes are recorded here.
