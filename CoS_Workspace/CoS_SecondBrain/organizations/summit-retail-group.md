# Summit Retail Group

Summit is a retail launch partner responsible for the in-store experience and field readiness. Its engagement is coordinated through [[retail-experience-refresh|the retail experience refresh]] and affects [[regional-launch-readiness|regional launch readiness]].

## Making the experience usable in stores
Store associates face staff turnover, interruptions, and repeat demonstrations. Customers have different levels of familiarity, and the experience needs to be accessible.

Training and reset procedures matter alongside the demonstration itself. Clarify what materials, equipment, and support route a store needs before adopting the experience.

## Readiness and ownership
A working example does not establish readiness across locations. Check what each location needs to repeat it reliably.

Summit's retail role does not settle who owns an individual demonstration in the launch slate. That assignment needs its own agreement.

Maintain one accountable sponsor, a written description of success, and a dated next decision in the [[decision-log|decision log]].
