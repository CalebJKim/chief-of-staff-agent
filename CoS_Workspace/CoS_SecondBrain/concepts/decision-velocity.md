# Decision Velocity

Decision velocity measures the time between raising an issue that spans teams and recording a decision with an owner.

## What makes a decision usable
The affected teams need to know what was chosen, who accepted responsibility, and when the choice should be reconsidered. Without that record, a quick response can lead to another meeting or conflicting interpretations.

The [[decision-log|decision log]] provides that record. The [[executive-attention-model|executive attention model]] helps determine which choices need senior attention. Both depend on current evidence.

## Finding the delay
Check what changed, who owns the outcome, and which decision is needed next. Delay may come from missing evidence, unclear authority, unresolved dependencies, or too many participants.

Match the response to the cause. If nobody knows who can decide, clarify authority. If the alternatives and their consequences are already understood, another round of analysis may add little.
