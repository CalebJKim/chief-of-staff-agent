# Q4 Operating Plan

The quarterly plan needs to match the work we want to do with the people and review time available. The current comparison focuses on customer onboarding, developer education, and internal documentation.

The plan remains at risk in the recorded notes. The next step is to confirm ownership, success checks, and the next decision date through [[weekly-leadership-staff]].

## The three work packages
- Customer onboarding: reusable deployment briefs, access checklists, acceptance questions, and support handoffs. Meridian's proposed evaluation and Northstar's recovery need different versions of this material.
- Developer education: a workshop, guided integration example, troubleshooting clinic, and route to support.
- Internal documentation: maintained runbooks, document owners, correction routes, and support references.

All three can reduce repeated work. They also need some of the same engineering and support reviewers.

## Compare the order of work
| Option | Benefit | Cost to make visible |
|---|---|---|
| Maintain the full scope | Preserves the intended breadth of outcomes | Requires credible staffing and dependency assumptions |
| Sequence work into stages | Reduces simultaneous demand on scarce skills | Some benefits arrive later |
| Protect core obligations and defer exploration | Creates capacity for delivery and risk reduction | Delays learning and potential future opportunities |

These are options under discussion, not an approved sequence.

## What the decision needs
For each package, describe its outcome, required skills, dependencies, and what would be delayed if it moved. Include coaching, onboarding, and review time rather than treating all staff time as project capacity.

Use [[finance-operating-plan]] for resource assumptions and [[workforce-plan]] for capability coverage. Explain why work is deferred and what would trigger reconsideration. Record accepted choices in [[decision-log]]. No new budget, staffing allocation, or delivery date is implied.
