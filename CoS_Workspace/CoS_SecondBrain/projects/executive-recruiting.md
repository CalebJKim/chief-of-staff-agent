# Executive Recruiting

This project defines the leadership role needed before evaluating candidates. The existing work includes role scope, location, and compensation constraints, but no candidate selection or offer is recorded here.

## Scope options being discussed
[[workforce-plan]] identifies needs in enterprise onboarding and partner enablement.

An onboarding-focused role would connect customer requirements, technical boundaries, acceptance evidence, and the support transition. An enablement-focused role would connect reusable examples, developer education, partner questions, and maintained documentation.

Decide whether those needs belong in one role before combining them into an overly broad job description.

## What the role brief needs
Explain the business problem, the decisions the role would own, its reporting relationships, and the people it must influence without managing directly. Separate essential experience from a preferred career path.

Different interviewers should assess different areas using a shared rubric: judgment, execution, team development, and cross-functional leadership.

## Proposed interview questions
Ask for examples of an ambiguous handoff, conflicting stakeholder expectations, and a process the candidate built that others could run.

A practical case could present an incomplete deployment packet. Ask the candidate to identify missing decisions, clarify responsibilities, and propose the next discussion. Assess the reasoning, not just presentation polish.

## Before a search or offer
Resolve scope and support needs alongside location and compensation. Keep candidate names, interview judgments, and compensation details in restricted recruiting records.

Coordinate with [[leadership-succession]], review unresolved choices in [[weekly-leadership-staff]], and record accepted commitments in [[decision-log]]. The role options above are proposals, not approved hiring decisions.
