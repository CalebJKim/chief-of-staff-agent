# Customer Advisory Council

The council brings customer experience into roadmap and deployment discussions. It should help the team understand different needs, not create the appearance that every customer wants the same thing.

## Proposed session
Start with a real workflow problem rather than a finished roadmap slide. Ask participants what triggers the work, how they handle it now, who is involved, and what happens when it fails.

Prepare a short problem statement and contrasting approaches. Explore control, setup effort, and operating cost. A regulated deployment may need a different answer from a small exploratory pilot.

## How to capture the discussion
Separate:
- Themes repeated across customers.
- Needs specific to a customer segment.
- Individual requests.
- Ideas that need more investigation.

Keep the context behind a comment and obtain permission before attributing it publicly. Interest in an idea is not a delivery commitment.
