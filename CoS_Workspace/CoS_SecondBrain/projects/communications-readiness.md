# Communications Readiness

This project keeps messages to employees, analysts, press, and customers consistent. Each audience needs different detail, but changing the format must not change the facts or imply an approval that has not happened.

## Packet being prepared
The proposed packet contains:
- A plain-language description of the user problem and workflow.
- A source map for factual statements.
- Explanations of unfamiliar terms.
- Audience-specific questions and answers.
- A list of facts or disclosure decisions still needing review.

Employee material would explain why the work matters and where questions go. Analyst material would explore positioning, adoption, and appropriate roadmap questions. Public wording needs its own factual and disclosure review.

## Who reviews what
[[jordan-kim]] coordinates the discussion. The checklist still needs to identify who verifies each statement, who decides whether it may be shared, and who maintains the approved version. Coordination does not replace technical, legal, or partner approval.

For incident messages, separate what is known, what is being investigated, and when another update can reasonably be given.

## Keep research questions open
If the audience challenges a claim, better phrasing may not solve the problem. Send the evidence question to [[analyst-relations-program]] or the relevant owner.

Coordinate with [[marketing-claims-rollout]] and [[board-readiness-program]]. Confirm next decisions through [[weekly-leadership-staff]] and [[decision-log]]. This work adds no new product benchmark or performance comparison.
