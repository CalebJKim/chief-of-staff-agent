# Leadership Succession

This project prepares for gaps in critical leadership roles. It covers both keeping decisions moving during an unexpected absence and developing people for larger responsibilities over time. Those are different goals.

## Two views to prepare
The continuity view asks who can cover essential work temporarily, what authority they need, and where knowledge is concentrated in one person.

The development view asks what experience could prepare someone for a broader role. Temporary coverage is not a promised promotion, and readiness depends on the role being considered.

## Development options under discussion
The onboarding and partner-enablement needs in [[workforce-plan]] offer possible assignments:
- Coordinate a deployment brief, open acceptance questions, and support handoff.
- Bring an integration example through documentation review and a troubleshooting discussion.
- Lead a budget tradeoff, customer escalation, or cross-team project with appropriate support.

Look for evidence that dependencies were identified, responsibilities clarified, and decisions raised with understandable alternatives. Finishing a document alone does not show readiness to lead the whole function.

## What to record
For each role, describe the decisions it owns, important relationships, specialized knowledge, and transition risks. The general planning note can show coverage needs without naming candidates or exposing private assessments.

Compare the risk of no backup with the time needed for development. Revisit the plan when the role changes. [[executive-recruiting]] covers an external hiring option. Confirm agreed actions through [[weekly-leadership-staff]] and [[decision-log]]. No successor or individual assessment is established here.
