# Talent and Succession Review

This review covers critical roles, possible successors, retention risks, and hiring needs.

## Before the meeting
Use [[workforce-plan|the workforce plan]], changes in [[leadership-succession|leadership succession]], and open commitments from the [[decision-log|decision log]].

Describe the capabilities the operating plan needs, current role coverage, and development gaps. Keep short-term continuity separate from preparation for a broader leadership role. Individual assessments need appropriate confidentiality and should stay out of general portfolio summaries.

## Current discussion
The working agenda compares coverage for enterprise onboarding and partner enablement. Define the responsibilities, work left uncovered, and coaching needed for an internal development assignment.

Compare development, reassignment, staged work, and recruitment. Include manager time and onboarding demands, not just headcount. A recruiting proposal needs a defined role scope.

## Outcomes to record
Record agreed development or staffing actions at the appropriate access level. Include the owner, rationale, date, and next checkpoint.

The intended outputs are a direction for role definition and questions to pursue about development or coverage. No individual assessment, candidate selection, successor appointment, or hiring approval is recorded here. Supporting material belongs in the workforce and succession notes and [[executive-recruiting|executive recruiting]].
