# Board Package Review

This review finalizes the board package's narrative, metrics, risks, and requests for board action.

## Before the meeting
Bring the current [[board-readiness-program|board readiness status]], changes from [[finance-operating-plan|the finance operating plan]], and open commitments from the [[decision-log|decision log]].

Check that the narrative, financial view, and risk discussion use consistent definitions and reporting periods. For each proposed board request, prepare the alternatives, assumptions, and consequence of deferring the decision. Separate those requests from information supplied for oversight.

## Operating-plan comparison
The proposed background packet compares customer onboarding, developer education, and internal documentation. Show the intended outcome, specialist time required, and ongoing maintenance for each.

Explain which work waits if the team stages the packages and how parallel work would compete for shared engineering and support reviewers. The finance plan records resource drivers; [[q4-operating-plan|the Q4 operating plan]] explains what each option protects or delays. Neither establishes approved spending.

## Decisions and follow-up
Decide what the package should ask the board to consider and which gaps must close before distribution. The comparison may belong in an oversight update or may need a specific decision request.

Record each decision's owner, rationale, date, and next checkpoint. Editing the package does not establish substantive agreement or board approval. No budget, financial forecast, or board outcome is recorded by this preparation note.
