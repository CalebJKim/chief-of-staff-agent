# Global Sales Forecast Review

This review reconciles regional forecasts and decides which strategic deals need executive sponsorship.

## Preparation
Bring changes since the previous [[global-sales-forecast|global sales forecast]] and the evidence behind them. Show opportunity value, expected timing, and confidence separately.

Identify dependencies shared by several deals, including changes in [[supply-allocation-plan|the supply allocation plan]]. Check open commitments in the [[decision-log|decision log]].

## Decisions
Challenge assumptions and identify where executive involvement could change an outcome. Forecast categories should reflect customer evidence, regardless of how important a target is.

Keep commercial risk separate from supply risk. A request for allocation does not confirm a sale. Scenarios discussed in the meeting remain separate from the committed forecast until explicitly adopted.

## Record
For each decision, capture the owner, rationale, date, and next checkpoint so the next review can distinguish new evidence from an unchanged assumption.
