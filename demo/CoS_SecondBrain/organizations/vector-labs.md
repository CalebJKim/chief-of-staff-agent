# Vector Labs

Vector is a strategic software partner for the Hermes and RTX Spark launch. The engagement runs through [[hermes-partner-program|the Hermes partner program]] and affects [[partner-certification|partner certification]].

## Defining the contribution
Both organizations should be able to explain which customer workflow improves and what each contributes. A clear outcome and operating model can be more useful than a long list of integrations.

Evidence from a pilot should retain its original scope when used in a broader presentation.

## Agreements to review
Technical compatibility, support ownership, commercial terms, and joint communications may progress at different rates. Review them separately so readiness in one area does not imply agreement in another.

Certification helps define the evidence and scope of an integration. Separate agreements are still needed to deliver or publicly describe the combined offering.

Maintain one accountable sponsor, a written success outcome, and a dated next decision in the [[decision-log|decision log]].
