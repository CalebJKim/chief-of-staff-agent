# Meridian Financial

Meridian is a strategic account exploring an internal policy-search workflow for operations staff. The proposed collection includes procedure manuals, escalation guidance, FAQs, and revision notices.

A user would inspect an answer and its source before applying it. The proposal excludes transaction processing and customer-facing financial advice.

## Who needs to be involved
The discussion needs input from customer operations, document owners, identity and access administrators, and support. These are functions to involve, not a list of confirmed customer approvers.

A business sponsor may support the idea while document or access questions remain unresolved. Keep those approvals separate.

## Material for the next discussion
Bring a proposed document list, sample questions, and a map of who would handle:
- An outdated or contradictory policy.
- A document the user is not allowed to read.
- A missing citation or unsupported answer.
- A connection problem or unavailable source.
- A correction that needs to reach saved copies.

The customer needs to decide what evidence would be enough to accept the initial workflow. A limited evaluation may answer that question without committing to a broad rollout.
