# Brightforge Studios

Brightforge is a creative production partner supporting the launch campaign and keynote assets. The engagement is coordinated through [[marketing-shoot|the marketing shoot]] and affects [[communications-readiness|communications readiness]].

## Preparing the brief
The production team needs a clear brief, usable source material, and a review process. Changes to claims or audience can affect the script, capture requirements, and editing even when the visual direction stays the same.

Track the source material used in each asset so a revision can reach the other versions that depend on it.

## Reviews and scheduling
Separate creative approval from factual review, production logistics, and permission to publish. When comparing shoot options, include time for review and rework.

A preferred concept or date still needs booking confirmation and final approval. Keep one accountable sponsor, a written description of success, and a dated next decision in the [[decision-log|decision log]].
