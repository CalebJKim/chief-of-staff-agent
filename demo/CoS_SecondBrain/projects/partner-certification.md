# Partner Certification

This project defines what a partner must demonstrate before an integration receives a particular approval. Technical compatibility, support readiness, security review, and permission to use a public badge are separate checks.

## Proposed evidence packet
Ask for the intended workflow, required configuration, dependencies, setup steps, expected observable result, limitations, and route for reporting problems.

| Review area | Evidence to assemble |
|---|---|
| Reproduction | A reviewer can follow the setup guide without undocumented assistance |
| Access boundaries | Required permissions and expected denial behavior are explained |
| Failure handling | Unavailable-source and invalid-configuration paths are understandable |
| Support ownership | The partner and internal team agree how questions are routed |
| Maintenance | Versions, review triggers, and the correction route are visible |

These are proposed requirements, not completed certification results.

## After the first review
Identify what changes would require another review. A once-valid result can become outdated if the version, integration boundary, or support arrangement changes.

Give partners a clear way to ask questions and report defects. Make exceptions visible rather than hiding them behind a single pass/fail label.

## How this relates to the demo and workshops
The packet can support the guided examples in [[developer-ecosystem-expansion]] and [[hermes-partner-program]]. Educational suitability still differs from certification.

A public badge, commercial agreement, approved statement, or customer-support promise needs its own decision. Confirm ownership through [[weekly-leadership-staff]] and record accepted commitments in [[decision-log]].
