# Supply Allocation Plan

This project decides how constrained supply should be distributed among customers, retail, developers, and regions. A request's urgency needs to be considered alongside existing obligations and whether the recipient is ready to use the allocation.

## Working request list
Capture the intended use, requested timing, deployment readiness, existing commitment, alternatives, and consequence of delay. Keep confirmed stock separate from expected deliveries with uncertain timing.

The list can distinguish contractual needs, customer recovery, strategic evaluations, retail preparation, and internal development. Those categories help discussion; they are not a fixed ranking that overrides a specific obligation.

## Handling exceptions
An exception should explain what it would displace and who must accept that consequence. Record the reason and when it should be reviewed again. A one-off senior request should not quietly become a permanent entitlement.

Allocating stock to someone who cannot deploy it yet may delay another customer without improving the first outcome. Equally, a purely short-term rule can harm a longer-term relationship.

## When availability changes
Show which commitments are affected, which requests can move, and which substitutes need confirmation. Coordinate [[global-sales-forecast]] and [[regional-launch-readiness]] so tentative allocations are not reported as fulfilled commitments.

Review ownership through [[weekly-leadership-staff]] and keep agreements in [[decision-log]]. The recorded plan remains at risk; no new quantities or delivery guarantees are added here.
