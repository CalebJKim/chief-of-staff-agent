# Hermes Partner Program

The program explains how partners could use Hermes in an agent workflow. The executive presentation should lead with a useful example and the next decision being requested, not a long list of platform features.

## What the review needs
- The Windows pilot smoke-check and operating evidence, with the scope of that evidence explained.
- The partner's intended benefit and the specific next step being requested.
- Whether [[openshell|OpenShell]] is ready to serve as an example of execution controls.
- A clear connection to the [[rtx-spark-launch|launch]] and [[agent-first-storyline|proposed keynote story]].

[[sofia-alvarez]] coordinates the VP review. [[aisha-rahman]] owns the presentation flow.

## Proposed partner materials
Prepare a workflow explanation, integration outline, participant guide, troubleshooting route, and support handoff. Explain where partner-specific work begins and which assumptions need another review before the example is reused.

The learning journey in [[developer-ecosystem-expansion]] runs from a workshop to a guided example and support. [[partner-certification]] asks a different question: what evidence shows that a particular integration can be reproduced, supported, and maintained?

## Decisions that must stay separate
A useful teaching example is not automatically certified. A successful technical test does not establish commercial agreement. Neither gives permission to make a joint public statement.

The next review should clarify the audience, each organization's proposed contribution, operating responsibilities, and material that can be shared. No new partner obligation, compatibility guarantee, or product-performance claim is added by this preparation.
