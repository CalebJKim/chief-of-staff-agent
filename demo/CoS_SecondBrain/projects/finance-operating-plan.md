# Finance Operating Plan

This project connects financial assumptions to the work they support. It covers forecast changes, program investment, hiring capacity, and downside planning. Explain how each financial change affects delivery.

The recorded plan is active. Ownership, success measures, and the next decision date still need confirmation through [[weekly-leadership-staff]].

## Explain forecast changes
Start with the prior view and separate changes caused by demand, price or mix, timing, staffing, and scope. A correction to the spreadsheet is different from an improvement in the business.

Lower spending could mean efficiency, but it could also mean delayed hiring or unfinished work. Show future obligations as well as the next payment. For staffing, distinguish approved positions, filled roles, expected starts, and capacity after onboarding.

## Resource comparison under discussion
| Package | Resource drivers to examine | Potential hidden demand |
|---|---|---|
| Customer onboarding | Solution design, control review, support transition | Senior specialists repeatedly resolving the same questions |
| Developer education | Example preparation, instruction, workshop support | Maintenance when interfaces or prerequisites change |
| Internal documentation | Authoring, review, document stewardship | Ongoing correction and retirement of outdated material |

These are the packages in [[q4-operating-plan]], not approved spending amounts.

## Compare realistic scenarios
Test related changes together. For example, slower customer deployment could affect revenue timing, support demand, and expansion investment. Avoid changing every line by the same percentage without a reason.

For each option, explain the work protected, delayed, or removed. Separate reversible adjustments from commitments that are difficult to undo. Use [[board-readiness-program]] where the decision needs board context, and record accepted commitments in [[decision-log]]. No new budget, savings estimate, or revenue figure is supplied here.
