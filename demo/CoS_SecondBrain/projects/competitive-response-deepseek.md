# Competitive Response — DeepSeek

This project considers what the DeepSeek Flash announcement means for positioning, model support, performance evaluation, and partner messaging. The aim is to understand which changes matter to customers before reacting.

## Start with the comparison
Identify the workload, deployment conditions, and customer problem being discussed. Differences in model settings, hardware, or evaluation method can make headline comparisons misleading.

Separate reproducible observations from third-party statements. Ask whether the change affects compatibility, cost, user experience, or mainly how the market perceives the offering.

## Choices to consider
A message update and a product investment are different decisions. A clearer explanation may address confusion without changing the roadmap. A genuine customer need may justify investigation even if it is not the loudest part of the announcement.

Would the proposed response help a customer, or merely copy a competitor? What evidence would change the recommendation?
