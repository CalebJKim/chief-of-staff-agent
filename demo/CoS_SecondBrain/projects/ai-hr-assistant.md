# AI-HR Assistant

AI-HR Assistant is a fictional employee self-service product, separate from the AI for Financial Analysis assistant.

## Internal pilot
The pilot is approved, and the user's access is enabled. Participants can update their home address, change ESPP contributions, and adjust 401(k) contributions. They review and confirm each proposed change before submission. This describes pilot capabilities; it does not record any changes to the user's information or contributions.

[[samira-noor|Samira Noor]] and HR coordinate the pilot and support. Participation is optional and there is no setup or update due today.
