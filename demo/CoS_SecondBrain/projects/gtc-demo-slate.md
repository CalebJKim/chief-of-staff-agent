# GTC Demo Slate

This note tracks the proposed list of demos for GTC. The list should show useful, different examples of local AI assistance. Final selection belongs in the [[rtx-spark-executive-review|executive review]].

## Candidates in the current notes
- An agent workflow using the [[hermes-partner-program|Hermes partner program]].
- A local inference example supported by the recorded [[inference-performance-claims|product evidence]].
- A secure-execution example using [[openshell|OpenShell]], subject to the open network-access decision.
- A retail experience, with the final responsible owner still to be confirmed through [[grant-walker]].

These are candidates, not approved selections.

## Compare the options
For each candidate, prepare a short entry covering:
- The user's problem and the benefit the example is intended to show.
- The request, information used, and output the audience would see.
- What the person reviews or decides.
- The presenter being proposed, preparation needed, and unresolved questions.
- How it differs from the other candidates.

A demo with polished visuals can still be unready if no one has accepted responsibility or a required setup step is unresolved.

## Prepare without deciding in advance
Before the review, gather source references, setup instructions, and an explanatory fallback. Assess whether the audience can follow the example and whether it can recover from an interruption.

During the review, select the demos and confirm assignments. Record accepted decisions in [[decision-log]]. A suggested presenter, program lead, and final accountable owner may be different people.

## Open blocker
The retail demo has no confirmed final owner. The proposed resolution is to settle that assignment in the executive review. This note does not resolve it or add any new product capability or performance claim.
