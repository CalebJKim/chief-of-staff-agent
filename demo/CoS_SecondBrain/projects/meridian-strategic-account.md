# Meridian Strategic Account

Meridian is considering a way for its operations staff to find answers in approved internal policies. A staff member would ask a procedural question, receive an answer with source links, and open the relevant passage before using it.

The proposed starting point is a limited collection of procedure manuals, escalation guides, FAQs, and revision notices. It does not include processing transactions, giving customers financial advice, or changing business systems automatically.

## What needs to be prepared
The account discussion needs a clear plan before anyone commits to a deployment:
- A list of documents and who decides which versions are authoritative.
- A map of who may access each source.
- Example questions and the evidence needed to judge the answers.
- A support plan for missing documents, permission problems, and incorrect answers.
- A technical design showing identity, data movement, logging, and retained copies.

The project connects to the [[hermes-partner-program|partner program]] and [[global-sales-forecast|sales forecast]], but interest in the proposal is not a purchase or deployment commitment.

## Questions to test
| Example | What it should help the reviewers decide |
|---|---|
| A straightforward policy question | Can the reader follow the answer back to the right passage? |
| A manual and FAQ disagree | Who identifies the source that should take precedence? |
| The user cannot access a document | Does the workflow respect that restriction? |
| No document answers the question | Is the gap made clear instead of an answer being assumed? |
| A policy has been replaced | Can the reader tell which version is current? |

## Choosing the initial scope
A small collection makes it easier to check permissions, sources, and support responsibilities. A broader integration may better resemble eventual use, but requires more teams and systems to be ready together.

Before selecting a scope, confirm the accountable owner, success checks, and next decision date through [[weekly-leadership-staff]]. Map unresolved questions to the function that can answer them. Record accepted commitments in [[decision-log]]; no customer approval or deployment date is established here.
