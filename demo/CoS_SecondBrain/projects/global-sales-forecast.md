# Global Sales Forecast

This project improves the explanation behind the sales forecast and identifies where senior involvement could help a strategic deal. Customer evidence matters more than how important an opportunity is to the target.

## Explain each change
Separate changes in value, timing, confidence, and scope. Record the customer's decision process and the technical or commercial steps still ahead. A supportive contact does not necessarily mean procurement or deployment is ready.

Build a clear comparison with the prior forecast so reviewers can understand what moved and why.

## Look for shared risks
Several opportunities may depend on the same supply, integration, or partner approval. Those risks are connected, not independent.

For each opportunity needing attention, distinguish missing customer evidence from an internal action. A senior conversation should help settle a specific issue, such as the acceptance process, rather than replace normal qualification.

## Watchlist for the review
Capture the next customer milestone, its evidence, the unresolved dependency, and what would change the forecast treatment. Keep customer-stated timing separate from internal targets.

Connect [[meridian-strategic-account]], [[regional-launch-readiness]], and [[supply-allocation-plan]]. A requested allocation is not a confirmed sale. Review ownership and next decisions through [[weekly-leadership-staff]] and record commitments in [[decision-log]]. No new deal values, dates, or probabilities are supplied here.
