# Developer Ecosystem Expansion

This project helps developers move from hearing about a capability to building and maintaining a useful integration. Downloads and event attendance show interest, but they do not show whether developers can complete the work.

## Proposed learning journey
1. Introductory workshop: explain the user problem, required setup, access limits, and where maintained instructions live.
2. Guided example: show source access, a bounded action or lookup, visible results, and an understandable failure path.
3. Troubleshooting clinic: work through missing permissions, unavailable sources, and stale configuration.
4. Support handoff: identify who maintains the material and where participants get help afterward.

The example is teaching material, not a guarantee of product behavior.

## Materials to prepare
A facilitator outline, participant setup guide, annotated example, diagnostic worksheet, and short support-reference page. Separate required setup from optional extensions so a participant knows when the core example is complete.

The same engineering and support reviewers may also be needed for customer onboarding and internal documentation. Their review capacity is a dependency, not an unlimited resource.

## How to judge usefulness
Look at where developers get stuck, whether they can find the right instructions, and whether examples remain usable after changes. Distinguish a common setup barrier from a request specific to one partner.
