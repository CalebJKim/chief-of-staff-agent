# Regional Launch Readiness

This project checks whether regional teams have the materials, training, approvals, and support needed for launch. Translating a global presentation is not the same as being ready to sell, deliver, or support the experience locally.

## What can differ by region
Channels, customer expectations, language, review processes, and support availability may all vary. Make those differences visible without changing the underlying product promises.

For each region, separate a centrally delivered input from local acceptance. An asset can be available while sales training, legal review, or support preparation is still incomplete.

## Questions for the readiness review
- Which materials and approvals are ready?
- What training or operational preparation remains?
- Which customer commitments depend on another team?
- What is the consequence of a local exception or delay?
- Who can settle the unresolved decision?

Explain the affected audience and consequence rather than hiding exceptions in one overall score.

The work connects [[rtx-spark-launch]] and [[global-sales-forecast]]. A global milestone does not imply identical conditions everywhere. Confirm the owner, success checks, and next decision date through [[weekly-leadership-staff]], and record accepted commitments in [[decision-log]].
