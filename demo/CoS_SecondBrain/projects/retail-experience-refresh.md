# Retail Experience Refresh

This project prepares the RTX Spark experience for a store setting: the demo, script, associate training, and troubleshooting. A presentation that works on stage may need changes for a short customer visit or repeated use throughout a day.

## What the store experience needs
The customer benefit should be easy to explain. Associates need setup and reset instructions, a clear route for problems, and a way to explain the limits of the example.

Consider interruptions, different levels of staff experience, accessibility, and handling of customer information. If a feature is unavailable, the associate needs an understandable alternative rather than an improvised claim about what the product can do.

## Preparation checks
- Can someone new to the example follow the instructions?
- Is the difference between the ideal path and a fallback clear?
- Are training and support material ready for reuse?
- Who is responsible for the particular demo, not just the overall retail program?
