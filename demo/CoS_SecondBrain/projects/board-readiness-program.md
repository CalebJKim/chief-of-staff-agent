# Board Readiness Program

This project prepares a board package that explains business performance, major risks, and decisions needing board attention. Collecting slides is only part of the job: the figures, time periods, assumptions, and requests need to agree.

## What each request should show
State the decision, the recommendation, alternatives, and the cost of waiting. Pair a metric with its definition and explanation of change. An improving number can hide a staffing problem; a short-term decline may reflect an intentional investment.

Keep information supplied for oversight separate from an explicit request for approval.

## Operating-plan comparison
The proposed background packet compares customer onboarding, developer education, and internal documentation. Show the outcome each seeks, the specialist time required, and the ongoing maintenance work.

[[finance-operating-plan]] describes resource assumptions without assigning new budgets. The launch context remains in [[rtx-spark-launch]]. A staged approach can reduce competing demands; parallel preparation may reveal gaps sooner but needs credible reviewer availability.

## Before distribution
Check for conflicting definitions, reporting periods, and financial assumptions. Keep unanswered questions visible rather than making the package look more certain than the evidence allows.

The program remains at risk in the recorded notes. Confirm its owner, success checks, and next decision date through [[weekly-leadership-staff]]. Record commitments in [[decision-log]]. Finishing the package does not establish that the board has approved its contents.
