# Marketing Shoot

The planned venue is unavailable. The recorded replacement options from [[priya-nair]] are Studio B on Friday and Studio C on Tuesday. A date needs to be chosen before the venue holds expire.

## The tradeoff
| Option | What it helps | What it makes harder |
|---|---|---|
| Friday at Studio B | Keeps the original production sequence | Leaves less time to prepare |
| Tuesday at Studio C | Gives more preparation time | Leaves less time for editing and revisions |

These are recorded planning options, not proof that either hold is still available. Reconfirm availability before requesting a booking.

## What to check before choosing
The venue is only one part of the plan. Compare crew and presenter availability, equipment needs, script readiness, and editing capacity. Choosing a date that preserves one step can still create pressure elsewhere.

The script needs the final [[inference-performance-claims|approved product wording]] and should support the [[rtx-spark-launch|launch story]]. A date decision does not approve the script or its claims.

## Keep the checkpoints separate
First choose the preferred date. Then confirm the booking and practical arrangements. Review the content through its own approval process.

The replacement date is still an open decision in these notes. Once it is settled, record what was chosen and the effect on preparation and editing time so the production team can plan the remaining work.
