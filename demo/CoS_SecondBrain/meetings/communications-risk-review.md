# Communications Risk Review

This review covers external claims, press positioning, employee messages, and readiness to answer questions.

## Material to bring
Use [[communications-readiness|communications readiness]], changes affecting [[qualified-product-claims|qualified product claims]], and open commitments in the [[decision-log|decision log]].

Bring each message with its audience, channel, planned timing, evidence, and likely questions. Read headlines with the accompanying image, chart, or customer example, since those can change the impression.

## Briefing packet
The working packet contains a plain-language workflow explanation, terminology page, source map, and questions for each audience. Review employee, analyst, and public versions separately. Internal wording is not automatically suitable for external use.

For each substantive statement, identify the evidence, who reviews it, and what can be disclosed. The proposed additions are qualitative and contain no comparative performance or numerical benchmark claims.

Use [[analyst-relations-program|the analyst relations program]] to track questions that need research.

## Decisions to record
Record which versions and uses were reviewed, applicable disclosure limits, and unresolved checks. If facts remain unsettled, identify any interim wording separately from a final conclusion.

Each decision needs an owner, rationale, date, and next checkpoint. Approval and publication are separate events; neither is presumed here.
