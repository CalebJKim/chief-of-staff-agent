# Partner CEO Review

This review addresses the joint roadmap, public commitments, and launch dependencies with strategic partners.

## Before the meeting
Bring progress from [[hermes-partner-program|the Hermes partner program]], changes involving [[vector-labs|Vector Labs]], and open commitments from the [[decision-log|decision log]].

Explain the value to each organization, evidence of progress, dependencies, and the executive decision requested. Show technical feasibility, commercial terms, and public wording separately when they are at different stages.

## Proposed enablement discussion
The working packet connects a qualitative workflow explanation with a guided integration example, learning materials, and a support handoff. Identify each organization's possible contribution and the permissions needed to share source material or publish joint wording.

The workshop and troubleshooting sequence in [[developer-ecosystem-expansion|developer ecosystem expansion]] can help define the contribution. [[partner-certification|Partner certification]] covers separate questions about reproducibility, access limits, error handling, and maintenance.

An educational example is not certification evidence or a commercial or service commitment.

## Decisions and follow-up
Resolve tradeoffs that need executive agreement. Record accepted actions and their scope, further reviews, and which communications remain internal. Each decision needs an owner, rationale, date, and next checkpoint.

A scoped contribution proposal and a responsibility map are possible follow-ups. They remain proposed outputs, not accepted obligations or evidence that a partner has passed review.
