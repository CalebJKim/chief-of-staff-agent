# Product and Engineering Operating Review

This meeting reviews milestones, release confidence, quality, staffing, and technical dependencies.

## Preparation
Bring evidence from [[agent-security-prd|the Agent Security PRD]], changes in [[operational-resilience|operational resilience]], and open commitments in the [[decision-log|decision log]].

Describe capacity constraints and decisions blocking implementation. Show whether a requirement is written, approved, implemented, or validated. Progress at one stage may leave another unresolved.

## Proposed review cases
The Meridian document-reference evaluation raises questions about source versions, access, citations, and information handling over time. Northstar's support handoff asks how implementation knowledge reaches the team supporting the customer. These cases have different acceptance criteria.

Bring unresolved requirements, a proposed responsibility map, and the effect on specialist reviewers. The proposed source-unavailability exercise could expose gaps between the application, integration, and support teams.

## Decisions
Resolve scope, sequence, and ownership questions that affect execution. Review changes in confidence as well as dates. Specify what additional evidence would change a technical risk assessment.

Record decisions with an owner, rationale, date, and next checkpoint. Keep detailed acceptance criteria in the relevant project records. This agenda does not authorize product changes or record completed validation.
