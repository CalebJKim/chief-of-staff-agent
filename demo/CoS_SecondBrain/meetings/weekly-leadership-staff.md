# Weekly Leadership Staff

This is the recurring operating meeting for Product, Engineering, Marketing, Finance, Legal, and Partners.

## Agenda
- Decisions and changes affecting the launch schedule.
- Risks needing executive ownership.
- Project health across the [[project-portfolio|project portfolio]].
- Staffing and partner commitments.

Bring changes that affect more than one function, such as a disputed priority or demand for shared staff. Link detailed project material so the meeting can focus on choices instead of reconstructing each project's history.

## Current planning discussion
The proposed comparison covers customer onboarding, developer education, and internal documentation. Meridian's evaluation, Northstar's support handoff, and the workshop-to-support sequence show the specialist review each would need.

Identify where engineering, governance, and support teams share responsibilities. Compare staged work with parallel preparation, including what would wait and what evidence would justify revisiting the choice. [[q4-operating-plan|The Q4 operating plan]] holds that comparison.

## Recording decisions
Separate information sharing from items that need a choice. If two priorities need the same people, make the tradeoff explicit. Record accepted decisions and reasons for deferral in the [[decision-log|decision log]].

Follow-ups also belong in project records, especially [[rtx-spark-launch|the RTX Spark launch]] and [[agent-security-prd|the Agent Security PRD]]. Keep account decisions, staffing choices, and conference selection in their appropriate processes. Discussing linked work here does not authorize all of it.
