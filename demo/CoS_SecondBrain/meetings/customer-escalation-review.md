# Customer Escalation Review

This meeting resolves deployment sequencing, support coverage, and responsibilities that the working team cannot settle.

## Before the meeting
Bring the customer's reported impact, workarounds, investigation progress, and dependencies blocking recovery. Use [[northstar-customer-recovery|Northstar customer recovery]], changes from [[enterprise-support-model|the enterprise support model]], and open commitments in the [[decision-log|decision log]].

Internal activity alone does not show that the customer's experience has improved. Identify what would confirm improvement with the customer.

## Proposed Northstar discussion
The current issue is the handoff from implementation to support. Prepare the deployment profile, known limitations, proposed intake route, and unresolved routing questions. The handoff still needs review and acceptance.

Decide which function receives an access request, an incident, or a design-change question. Agree what information accompanies each and how support obtains missing context while keeping responsibility for the case.

A walkthrough of an access problem and an unavailable-source problem could reveal gaps in the proposed process.

## Follow-up
Separate immediate containment from permanent correction. Record the next customer communication point and remaining uncertainty.

The intended outputs are a reviewed handoff packet and a route for customer confirmation. Record decisions with an owner, rationale, date, and next checkpoint. Closing an internal escalation does not establish customer acceptance of recovery.
