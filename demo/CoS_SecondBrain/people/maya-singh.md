# Maya Singh

Maya is the retail experience director. She is accountable for [[retail-experience-refresh|the retail experience refresh]], which depends on choices in [[ifa-demo-slate|the IFA demo slate]].

## Designing for a store
Customers and store associates may be seeing the product for the first time. The experience needs to explain itself, recover from interruptions, and work for repeat use. Accessibility and everyday store constraints belong in the plan.

A stage presentation can assume a prepared presenter and an uninterrupted sequence. A store demonstration needs supporting training and a practical way to restart when someone leaves midway through.

## What to bring to a review
Describe the experience goal, the training needed, and how associates should handle a failed or interrupted demonstration. Explain the risk and any executive decision needed.

Maya's responsibility for the retail program does not establish that she has accepted a particular demo assignment. A named owner for the IFA slate still needs an explicit commitment. Coordinate through [[weekly-leadership-staff|weekly leadership staff]] and record commitments in the [[decision-log|decision log]].
