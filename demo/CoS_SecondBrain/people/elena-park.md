# Elena Park

Role: Executive program lead.

Elena coordinates the [[rtx-spark-executive-review]] and connects the work behind [[rtx-spark-launch]]. Her review material should explain how the separate tasks contribute to the launch and which decision is holding up the next step.

## What to bring to a discussion
Explain the consequence, options, recommendation, and decision needed. Make unclear ownership visible rather than assuming the coordinator will absorb it.

For the executive review, prepare the proposed story and demo choices before the meeting. Approval and accepted ownership belong in the meeting, not in the preparation checklist.

## Broader planning context
The operating-plan discussion compares customer onboarding, developer education, and internal documentation. Those packages may compete for the same specialist reviewers. Show what a staged sequence would protect or delay.

[[weekly-leadership-staff]] is the cross-functional coordination forum. [[decision-log]] records accepted commitments. Neither coordination nor a proposed plan establishes a new assignment to Elena or the user.
