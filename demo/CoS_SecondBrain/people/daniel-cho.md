# Daniel Cho

Role: Legal claims owner.

Daniel is the recorded contact for the wording and conditions attached to [[inference-performance-claims|product-performance statements]]. That work feeds [[marketing-claims-rollout]].

## What a review request needs
Provide the proposed wording, underlying result, test conditions, comparison baseline, and intended audience. Show where it will appear: a slide, chart, social caption, and retail script can create different impressions even when they repeat the same number.

Small edits matter. Removing a condition or changing “throughput” to a broader claim about speed can change the meaning.

## Keep approvals specific
An approved source statement is not blanket clearance for every later adaptation. Identify what changed and why it still needs review.

Daniel's recorded role is a route for interpreting claims, not proof that every campaign asset is approved. Coordinate through [[weekly-leadership-staff]] and record actual decisions in [[decision-log]].
