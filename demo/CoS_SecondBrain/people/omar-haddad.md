# Omar Haddad

Omar leads infrastructure and reliability. He is accountable for [[operational-resilience|operational resilience]], with dependencies on [[enterprise-support-model|the enterprise support model]].

## Incident and recovery discussions
Explain what users cannot do, what workaround is available, and what recovery depends on. Technical recovery may happen before users have a dependable experience again. Keep remaining uncertainty visible.

A written procedure and a procedure demonstrated in an exercise provide different levels of evidence. Infrastructure ownership also needs to connect to the customer-facing support route so an escalation has somewhere to go.

## Proposed source-availability exercise
The exercise follows a document source becoming unavailable. Distinguish a connection failure from denied access or a missing document, since each needs a different response.

The review should identify the first observable signal, where the failure occurs, and the diagnostic information needed to investigate. Define what evidence would establish that normal operation has returned, alongside the support team's customer-facing response.

Discussions with [[arcadia-cloud|Arcadia Cloud]] concern possible responsibilities. They do not establish a supplier assignment or a completed exercise. No availability or recovery-time guarantee is recorded.

Coordinate decisions through [[weekly-leadership-staff|weekly leadership staff]] and record commitments in the [[decision-log|decision log]].
