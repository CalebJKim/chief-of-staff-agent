# Wei Zhang

Wei leads the APAC business. He is accountable for [[regional-launch-readiness|regional launch readiness]], with dependencies on [[partner-certification|partner certification]].

## Translating the global plan
Regional readiness depends on local routes to market and the partners serving those markets. A centrally completed asset or certification step may leave local operating requirements unresolved.

Explain what a global decision means for the region. Identify the specific partner dependency and any exception that needs coordination.

## Checking readiness
Technical qualification does not, by itself, establish that a partner is ready to sell, deploy, or support an offering. Review those needs separately so the regional plan reflects actual operating conditions without making inconsistent product commitments.

Bring outcomes, risks, and decisions to [[weekly-leadership-staff|weekly leadership staff]]. Keep commitments current in the [[decision-log|decision log]].
