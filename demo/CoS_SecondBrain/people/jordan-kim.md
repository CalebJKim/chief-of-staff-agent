# Jordan Kim

Role: Corporate communications director.

Jordan connects [[communications-readiness]] with [[analyst-relations-program]]. Different audiences can receive different levels of detail, but the underlying facts need to stay consistent.

## Proposed briefing material
The packet includes a plain-language workflow description, terminology guide, source map, and question register. Employee context, analyst discussion, and public wording need separate review boundaries.

For each substantive statement, identify who verifies the fact, who permits disclosure, and who maintains the version being used. Jordan's coordination role does not replace those reviews.

## Questions that need more than editing
When an analyst asks for evidence, record the research question and send it to the appropriate owner. A request for clarification is not an endorsement.

Coordinate through [[weekly-leadership-staff]] and use [[decision-log]] for accepted commitments. No new benchmark or comparative product-performance statement is part of this packet.
