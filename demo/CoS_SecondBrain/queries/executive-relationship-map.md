## Using the map
The person who understands an issue, the person who can resolve it, and the person who needs consultation may differ. An executive sponsor is also distinct from the day-to-day project owner.

These links describe existing roles. They do not establish new assignments, accepted deliverables, or authority to commit another team. Involve people according to the outcome at issue; a project's needs can change without a change in anyone's formal role.
