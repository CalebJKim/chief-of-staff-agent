## Comparing work
The portfolio includes customer recovery and deployment, commercial planning, workforce development, security and reliability, partner adoption, and launch execution. Each needs different evidence of progress. A hiring decision, a tested control, and customer acceptance cannot be compared by counting activities alone.

Look for shared staffing limits and dependencies that affect several outcomes. Distinguish committed delivery from exploration and work intended to reduce risk. The table records project health; check current evidence before treating it as a live ranking or changing a commitment.

## Current planning comparison
Customer onboarding, developer education, and internal documentation compete for specialist review time.

The onboarding work has two reference cases. Meridian proposes a bounded policy-reference evaluation. Northstar needs a better handoff from implementation to support. Their acceptance conditions differ.

Developer education proposes a workshop, guided integration example, troubleshooting clinic, and support handoff. Internal documentation would support these efforts with maintained runbooks, correction routes, and named responsibility for upkeep.

The finance plan compares resource needs without assigning budgets. The workforce plan examines onboarding and enablement coverage without assuming new hires. Governance and resilience reviews add questions about document handling and failure response.

This comparison does not establish an approved delivery sequence or change the statuses above.
