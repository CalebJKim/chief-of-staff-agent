## Preparing a live brief
Separate new information from standing background, and preparation work from decisions needing executive judgment or relationship context. Several messages about one outcome do not necessarily create several priorities.

Keep unresolved work where it still matters. Use current evidence to avoid turning every historical dependency into an immediate task.
