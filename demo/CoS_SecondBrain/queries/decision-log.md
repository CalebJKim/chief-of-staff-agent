## Reading a decision
A recommendation, an accepted decision, and completed follow-up are separate stages. Approval may leave actions unfinished; preparing a document does not establish that the underlying decision was made.

Keep the alternatives considered and the reason for the choice alongside the record.

## Reconsidering a choice
When a decision changes, retain the earlier rationale and identify the new evidence or constraint. Record which projects and uses an approval covers, including any conditions.

The entries above retain their recorded status. This guidance does not create additional approvals.
