## Reading the table
The owner path points to a source of coordination or expertise. It does not necessarily establish that the person has accepted responsibility for resolving the entire risk.

A listed response remains proposed until there is evidence that it has been carried out and reduced the risk.

## Review questions
What would make the risk urgent? What would happen, and do other risks depend on the same people or resources?

Keep remaining exposure visible after an action finishes. Check the project decision record so a choice about scope, timing, or ownership is not mistaken for completed work.
