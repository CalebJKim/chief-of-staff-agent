# Launch Critical Path

The critical path is the chain of dependencies that determines whether the planned launch date is still achievable.

## Finding what controls the date
A short approval can hold up several longer activities. A delayed task may have little immediate effect if its schedule has room to move. Review dependencies before equating activity with progress.

For each dependency, identify what releases the next team to proceed and what happens if that event moves. An input being available is different from the next team accepting and using it.

## Keeping the plan current
Ask what changed, who owns the affected outcome, and what decision is needed next. Recheck the chain when scope or sequencing changes. A dependency that previously had spare time can become the one that controls delivery.

Use this approach for [[rtx-spark-launch|the RTX Spark launch]] and [[regional-launch-readiness|regional launch readiness]], with current evidence from the teams involved.
