# Inference Performance Claims

This note records the conditions attached to the existing product-performance statement. Those conditions determine where the claim applies.

## Recorded evidence
The approved package supports a 1.8x throughput statement for the validated model and batch configuration at the agreed power conditions. [[mike-chen]] owns the underlying package. [[daniel-cho]] owns the approved qualification of the wording.

Do not use the headline without those conditions. This note does not establish results for another model, setup, or workload.

## What the measures mean
- Throughput: how much output is produced over a period.
- Time to first output: how long someone waits before the response starts.
- Total response time: how long the whole request takes.
- Energy use: the energy required under the stated measurement conditions.

An improvement in one measure does not establish an improvement in another. For example, a throughput result should not be rewritten as a claim about how quickly every user receives an answer.

## Where the statement is used
Slide 4 holds the main executive wording. [[marketing-claims-rollout]] carries that wording into campaign materials. The evidence also supports the [[agent-first-storyline|proposed keynote story]] within its recorded scope.

When a claim is shortened or placed next to a chart, keep the workload, comparison, and limits understandable. A changed configuration may need new evidence, not simply a different caption.
