# Product Specifications

This note records the approved RTX Spark specifications and the wording to retain when using them in launch materials.

## Recorded evidence
[[mike-chen]] owns the approved specifications package:

- Blackwell RTX GPU: Up to 6,144 cores
- Grace CPU: Up to 20 cores
- FP4 AI performance: Up to 1 petaflop
- Unified memory: Up to 128 GB

[[daniel-cho]] has cleared this wording for leadership review. Keep “up to” with every specification and retain the FP4 precision label. Final external copy still needs Legal review.

These specifications do not establish measured results for a model or workflow. They do not support a tokens-per-second figure, an energy-saving percentage, or a comparison with a prior release.

## What the specifications describe
- GPU and CPU core counts describe different processors. Keep their names attached to the counts.
- FP4 identifies the precision for the AI performance specification. Dropping it broadens the meaning.
- Unified memory describes memory capacity. It is not a model-size or response-speed guarantee.

“Up to” is part of the approved wording, including the memory specification. A shorter caption must retain it.

## Where the statement is used
Slide 4 holds the main executive wording. [[marketing-claims-rollout]] carries that wording into campaign materials. The evidence also supports the [[agent-first-storyline|proposed keynote story]] within its recorded scope.

When a specification is shortened or placed next to a chart, keep its component, unit, and qualifier clear. A comparison or workflow claim needs separate evidence and review.
