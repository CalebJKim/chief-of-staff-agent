# Executive Sponsorship

Executive sponsorship gives a senior leader responsibility for removing an obstacle affecting a strategic customer, partner, or commitment shared across teams.

## When to involve a sponsor
A sponsor can help when the working group lacks authority to make a decision. The intervention may settle competing priorities or establish who is accountable for a shared outcome.

Start with what changed, who owns the result, and the decision needed next. Repeated senior status checks add work if there is no decision to make.

## Returning to normal ownership
Define the issue and when the regular owner should take over again. Sponsoring a decision does not require managing the project day to day.

For [[northstar-customer-recovery|Northstar customer recovery]] and [[meridian-strategic-account|the Meridian strategic account]], distinguish reassurance about the relationship from technical or contractual commitments that still need review. Use current customer evidence to decide where sponsorship would help.
