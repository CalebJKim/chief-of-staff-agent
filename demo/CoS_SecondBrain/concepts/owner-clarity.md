# Owner Clarity

An accountable owner is a person who has accepted responsibility for a defined outcome, has the authority to act, and knows when the next decision is due. This person is sometimes called the directly responsible individual, or DRI.

## What an assignment needs
The owner needs a practical way to get help from contributing teams. Naming someone in a document does not establish that they accepted the assignment or control its dependencies.

The coordinator, subject expert, and accountable owner may be different people. Similarly, the person tracking a blocker may not be able to remove it.

## Resolving an unclear assignment
Ask which result the person owns and which decisions belong elsewhere. For work shared across teams, define the handoffs rather than giving several people the same vague responsibility.

Apply this to [[ifa-demo-slate|the IFA demo slate]] and the [[project-portfolio|project portfolio]]. Check current evidence for what changed, who has accepted the outcome, and the next decision required.
