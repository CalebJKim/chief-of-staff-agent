# Operating Cadence

An operating cadence is the recurring set of meetings and written updates used to spot changes, resolve dependencies, and record commitments.

## Choosing the right meeting
Match the frequency and participants to the work. Collecting status, handling exceptions, planning, and making executive decisions may need different forums.

Putting everything into one meeting can leave little time for judgment. Information that people can read beforehand should not crowd out decisions.

## Following through
Use current evidence to establish what changed, who owns the result, and what needs to be decided. Record the outcome and carry unresolved dependencies forward without reconstructing the entire history each time.

Review a recurring meeting if it stops producing useful decisions or coordination. Its continued presence on a calendar is not evidence that it still serves its purpose.
